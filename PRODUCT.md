# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Novelist serves novelists and other long-form writers who want focused desktop
writing, trustworthy plain files, and first-class Chinese, Japanese, and Korean
input. They work in long sessions across manuscripts, notes, references, and
visual planning surfaces, and need the tool to stay out of the prose while
keeping advanced workflows discoverable.

## Product Purpose

Novelist provides a lightweight, local-first WYSIWYG Markdown workspace with
project organization, templates, visual planning, export, and extensibility.
Success means writers can move from project creation through sustained drafting
and review without surrendering file ownership, editor responsiveness, or CJK
correctness.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, direct, dependable. The product should feel like a stable writing desk:
polished enough for long daily use, restrained enough to keep the manuscript at
the center, and clear about what every uncommon action will do.

## Anti-references

- A heavy IDE whose chrome and configuration overwhelm the writing surface.
- A marketing-style app shell built from oversized decorative sections.
- A proprietary document silo that hides or rewrites the user's Markdown.
- A crowded feature drawer that makes canvas, kanban, mindmap, templates, or
  plugin workflows effectively undiscoverable.
- Novel interaction patterns where familiar desktop controls would be clearer.

## Design Principles

- Keep the manuscript visually primary and make the next writing action obvious.
- Earn complexity through progressive disclosure, without demoting core product
  capabilities.
- Preserve trust through stable layout, explicit consequences, and plain-file
  ownership.
- Prefer compact, familiar desktop interactions that remain comfortable during
  long sessions.
- Treat CJK behavior and readability as foundational product quality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target is currently declared. Interfaces must nevertheless
preserve keyboard access and visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion
behavior, and robust CJK typography and IME interaction. Controls and layouts
must remain usable in compact desktop windows without hiding primary actions.
